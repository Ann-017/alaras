"Flora! Ah… Ahahahaha!"

I began to laugh. Not because his words held the slightest trace of humor, but because I could scarcely bring myself to believe what I had just heard.

"Flora? My own sister?"

I looked at him, then slowly shook my head.

"I see… I see now that it was never much of a love affair on your part."

My laughter gradually faded.

"But allow me to tell you something, Tristan. I know you. I know your little secrets, your pretty lies, and every single thing you would rather see buried along with me. If I fall, you shall fall with me!"

His smile did not falter.

"Are you threatening me, Miss Saraphina? Would you care to see what I am capable of?"

I lifted my chin.

"Go ahead. Show me."

THUD!

A sudden impact, a sharp pain. I lowered my eyes, unable to understand what had just happened.

The knife… It was the very one he had taken from me.

He had done it. He had stabbed me. My legs gave way, and I fell to the ground.

"Remember one thing, Saraphina: I am not afraid of you. And you are no longer of any use to me."

He crouched slightly to look at me.

"In truth, I was the one who wrote you that letter. It was a trap. And do not worry… we shall make certain everyone knows what you have done."

I tried to speak. But somehow, I could not find the strength.

"I… I…"

The words tangled in my mouth. I could barely manage to form a complete sentence.

"I gave you everything… Everything… Tristan… How… how could you do this to me?"

There were so many things I wanted to tell him. So many accusations I wished to make. So many questions to which I knew, deep down, he would never give me an answer.

And yet no words seemed large enough to contain what I felt.

"Hm! I couldn't care less about how you feel."

He lowered himself to my level and said to my face:

"You are utterly pathetic."

Pathetic. Me?

I gathered what little strength remained in me and seized his collar by surprise.

"You will pay dearly for this, Tristan Fitzgerald Augustus… Even if I must descend into Hell itself to make you pay!"

Then I spat right in his face. With a sudden burst of strength, I raised my hand and struck his face.

SMACK!

The sound echoed through the area. Despite the pain, I felt a flicker of satisfaction as his head turned from the force of the blow.

I wanted that slap to leave a mark. A mark he would be forced to carry, whether he wished it or not. I wanted him to remember me every time he caught sight of his own reflection.

"Ah!"

He staggered, one hand flying to his face. After a few moments, he managed to straighten himself, visibly furious.

"I do not know why, but I cannot even bring myself to hate you…"

He paused, still rubbing his cheek.

"You have some spirit; I will grant that. And for that very reason, I shall not kill you myself. No… I would much rather leave you here to die, slowly."

Then he straightened completely.

"Pack everything up! And leave her here to suffer. As for anyone who dares reveal a single word of what has transpired… let them understand that their life, along with the lives of their families, shall hang by a thread."

And then they left.

They all left.

Without a backward glance.

Without even bothering to see whether I was still conscious.

I cannot say how much time has passed. Unable to rise, I attempted to crawl across the floor. Every movement demanded an extraordinary effort. Behind me, I left traces of my passage as my strength slowly abandoned me.

No. No, I could not die. Not like this. Not after everything I had sacrificed.

If I were to die, then I would do so only after obtaining my revenge. I was not ready to leave. Not yet. It was not fair. It was so terribly unfair!

I had given everything. My soul, my life, my reputation… Everything I was, everything I might have become. And for what? To be loved.

To be loved by a man who had never loved me as I had loved him.

So, this was what remained of seven years of waiting? Was this what all my promises, all my sacrifices, all those nights spent believing that tomorrow would finally be our day were worth?

A throne I would never wear.
A wedding that would never take place.

And a man who, tomorrow, would take my sister as his bride.

I continued to move, but my breathing grew shallow. My vision began to blur. The world around me seemed to drift away, as though an immense distance had suddenly risen between me and everything I had ever known.

My arms could barely support me any longer.

At last, I collapsed beneath my own weight, unable to go any further.

Was this truly how my life was meant to end?

I did not wish to die. No… I refused to die like this.

Hear me! Whoever you may be! God, the Devil… I don't care! I beg you, hear me! Grant me a chance. Just one. A chance to begin again, to set things right. I surrender myself to you, and to you alone. Take my soul, if that is the price I must pay.

But in return… Let me have my revenge. Let me make him pay for everything he has done to me. I beg you…

And then, darkness.

"You who have called upon me… I hear your prayers. I hear your lamentations. And I see your suffering."

A voice, deep, solemn, unlike anything I had ever heard.

"You seek a second chance. You wish to return to the beginning and reclaim the course of your existence. You desire to correct your mistakes, overturn your fate, and obtain the vengeance for which your heart yearns so desperately."

A silence followed, then the voice spoke once more.

"Very well. I shall grant it."

I could neither see nor understand the source of that voice. And yet, it seemed to come from everywhere at once.

"But know this, Saraphina Hawthorn: whatever path you choose, you shall bear the consequences of your actions. You shall not be spared the price of what you have done."

The voice grew deeper.

"You have taken a soul yourself. It is only fair that you pay with your own."

Another pause.

"A soul for a life. Twenty years for a soul. Vengeance in exchange for your soul."

Then, amidst the darkness, Death spoke the final words of its bargain:

"I grant you a second chance. But remember… every debt must, in time, be collected."
